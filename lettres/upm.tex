Dear Members of the Selection Committee,

\vspace{0.4cm}

I am currently a fourth-year Engineering student in Computer Science at
ENSEIRB-MATMECA (Bordeaux INP), and I am applying for an Erasmus+
exchange at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid during the second
semester of my academic year.

The Universidad Politécnica de Madrid is my preferred destination
because of its outstanding reputation in engineering and computer
science. The courses offered fit remarkably well with both my academic
curriculum and my personal interests, particularly in software
architecture, embedded systems, optimization algorithms, mobile
application development and digital signal processing.

My objective is to strengthen my software engineering skills while
deepening my understanding of the interaction between hardware and
software. Beyond the academic perspective, I am willing to discover a
different teaching environment, collaborate with students from diverse
backgrounds, and gain the experience of studying in an international
setting.

Since all the courses I selected are taught in English, I will be able
to fully benefit from the programme while continuing to improve my
technical and oral communication skills. I am comfortable working with
English documentation and following lectures in English.

This mobility naturally complements my curriculum at ENSEIRB-MATMECA.
The knowledge and experience gained at UPM will contribute directly to
my engineering education before I complete my mandatory internship in
France.

Studying at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid would represent an
important step in my academic and personal development. I am highly
motivated to contribute to your university community and make the most
of this opportunity.
